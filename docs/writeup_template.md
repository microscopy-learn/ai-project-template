# Write-up template (publish on your site, then share on LinkedIn)

Aim for 800 to 1,500 words, with one diagram and one results table.

1. **The problem (2 to 3 sentences).** Who has it, why it matters, what "good" looks like.
2. **What I built (1 paragraph + architecture diagram).**
3. **The interesting part.** One hard problem and how you solved it. This is what interviewers ask about.
4. **How I measured it.** Eval set, metrics, results table, what improved between versions.
5. **What didn't work.** Approaches you tried and dropped, with numbers.
6. **Trade-offs.** Quality vs cost vs latency; why you chose this design.
7. **What I'd do next.**
8. **Links.** Demo, repo, video.

LinkedIn post version (150 to 250 words): the problem in one line, a demo GIF, three lessons learned as short points, links to the repo and write-up.
